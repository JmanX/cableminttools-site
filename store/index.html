Quick questions

What exactly do I receive?

A digital ZIP download containing editable Excel (.xlsx) workbook(s) and setup instructions. No physical item is shipped.

Do I need a subscription?

No. These nine downloads are one-time purchases, separate from CableMint Field Tools Pro subscriptions.

Can I use Google Sheets?

These files are delivered as Excel workbooks. If you import them into Google Sheets, please check formulas and layout because conversion may change workbook behavior.

Can I buy through Etsy instead?

Yes. Every product includes a “Prefer Etsy?” link as an alternative to direct checkout.
