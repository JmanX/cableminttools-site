CableMint Account & Data Deletion

For CableMint Device Capture and CableMint Field Tools accounts.

You can request deletion of your CableMint account and the associated CableMint cloud data.

How to request deletion

Email support@cableminttools.com from the email address associated with your CableMint account and use the subject CableMint Account Deletion Request.

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted

After we verify the request, we will delete the CableMint account and associated CableMint-controlled cloud data, including applicable project records, captured device records, saved calculations, Gap/Punch List records, and stored project files/photos.

What may be retained

We may retain limited information when required for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes. Payment processors may retain transaction records according to their own legal and compliance obligations.

Subscriptions

Deleting a CableMint account does not automatically cancel obligations handled by an external payment provider. If you have an active subscription, contact support if you need help confirming its cancellation status before or as part of your deletion request.

Need help?

Contact support@cableminttools.com.
