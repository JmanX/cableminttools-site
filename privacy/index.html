Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains how CableMint Tools handles information when you use our website and Field Tools application.

Information we may collect

We may collect basic usage and analytics information such as pages viewed, calculator usage, device/browser information, referral source, and interactions with links. If paid accounts are introduced, we may also process account details such as name and email address.

Payments

Payment transactions are processed by our payment provider. CableMint Tools does not directly store full payment card details. Payment providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

Local app data

Some Field Tools features currently store project names, pinned tools, usage preferences, and saved calculations locally on your device using browser storage. This local data is not automatically uploaded to CableMint Tools.

Analytics

We use analytics services, including Google Analytics, to understand traffic and product usage and to improve the service.

How we use information

Information may be used to operate, secure, improve, analyze, and support CableMint Tools and to provide purchased or subscribed features.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate analytics, hosting, payments, or customer support, subject to their applicable terms and privacy obligations.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time and can clear locally stored app data through your browser or device settings.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the contact methods published on CableMintTools.com.
