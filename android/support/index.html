Device Capture Support

For help with your existing CableMint account, password recovery, the app, privacy, or account and data deletion, contact support.

Email support

Email support@cableminttools.com with the subject CableMint Device Capture Support.

Email CableMint Support

Include a short description of the issue and the email address associated with your account. Do not send passwords, recovery links, or session tokens.

If no email application opens, copy the address and subject into your usual email service.

Account help

  • Recover your password
  • Request account or data deletion
  • Read the Privacy Policy

You do not need to sign in, hold Pro access, or install the app to request support or deletion. Existing cancellation-status questions can also be sent to support.

If the app did not open

Open CableMint Device Capture from your installed apps. If it is not installed or the browser could not open it, you can still use the email and deletion options on these pages.

Return to CableMint Device Capture

Opening the app is optional. You can read these pages and request support or deletion without installing or opening the app.
