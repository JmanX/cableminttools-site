How to size a PoE switch

Device load: number of devices × maximum watts per device. Planning target: device load × (1 + headroom ÷ 100). Compare that target with your switch's total available PoE budget.

Example: 24 security cameras

If 24 cameras each draw up to 12 W, the total is 288 W. With 20% planning headroom, target 345.6 W. A switch offering 370 W of available PoE power meets that total-budget target, provided its individual ports also support the devices.

What else should you check?

  • Use manufacturer maximum draw, including IR illumination, heaters, or other peak loads where applicable.
  • Confirm the IEEE PoE standard and per-port power limit match each device.
  • Verify the quoted switch budget is available to powered devices, not only the switch's AC input rating.
  • Review cable length, wiring quality, voltage drop, and UPS runtime separately.

This is an estimate, not an electrical compliance determination or a replacement for manufacturer specifications.
